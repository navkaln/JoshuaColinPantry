# Editing the Griswold Hall site (for the board liaison and committee)

You edit in **Sanity Studio**, a simple web form. No code. (The Studio must be set up first. See `README.md`, "Sanity".)

## Change a page's text
1. Open the Studio and sign in.
2. Click the page name on the left (Home, The Building, Amenities, or Contact).
3. Click in a text box and type. Each box says which part of the page it controls.
4. Click **Publish**. The site updates after it rebuilds (usually a few minutes).

Boxes you leave empty keep the grey placeholder text. Once you fill one in, its yellow "Placeholder" tag disappears.

## Swap a photo
1. Open the page, find the photo field (for example "Facade photo").
2. Click **Upload** (or drag a file in). Photos at least 2000 pixels wide look best.
3. Type **alt text**: one plain sentence describing what the photo shows, for people who cannot see it. Required.
4. Click **Publish**.

## Please do not publish
Resident names, unit numbers, photos of identifiable people, co-op finances, meeting minutes, email addresses, dog run rules or hours (not yet approved), or anything about landmark status or solar panels (not yet confirmed). Content is approved by the board liaison before it is published.

## Something looks wrong?
Click the clock icon in a page to see its history and restore an earlier version.
